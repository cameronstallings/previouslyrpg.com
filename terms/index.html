Previously lets a tabletop RPG group ("a table") capture moments during a session, by short voice recordings or typed notes, and turns them into a written and narrated recap for the next session. Recaps are generated automatically by software from what your table captured. They can be wrong, incomplete, or odd. The game master can edit them. Do not rely on a recap for anything that matters outside the game.
