A recap, as it arrives

A sample recap · Session VII

The bridge at Harrow Ford

Previously, the company reached Harrow Ford at dusk and found the bridge held by three of the Ironhand's riders. Kael talked, badly; Wren did not talk at all, and by the time the riders understood the difference two of them were in the river. The third rode north with the news, which was the one outcome everyone had agreed to avoid.

The tollkeeper, Old Mab, fed them anyway, and mentioned, as if it were nothing, that the Ironhand's daughter had crossed the same bridge two nights before, alone, heading for the hills.

A sample. Yours is written from the moments your table captured, and read aloud by a narrator who has been taught how to say your names.
