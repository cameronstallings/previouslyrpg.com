For tabletop RPG tables

Every session, remembered.

Your table talks. Previously listens, and before the next session it tells the story back, written and read aloud.

Tap the button whenever something happens at the table. Between sessions, Previously turns those moments into a short "previously on" recap in the voice of a narrator, so nobody spends the first twenty minutes asking what happened last time.

Capture moments

Tap to record while the scene plays out, and tap again to stop. Or type it. Nothing is recorded unless you started it.

A recap, narrated

Before next session, a ninety-second recap arrives, written from your moments and read aloud.

Coming to the App Store
