How it works

At the table

Tap the seal and say what happened.

A line worth keeping, a turn nobody saw coming, the name of the innkeeper. One tap starts a short clip, you say it in a few words, and it is kept. Or type it. If the game master is recording the whole night, a tap marks the moment instead.

When the night ends

The GM ends the session.

Everything the table captured is written into one recap, told as a story, by the time you are home.

A recap, as it arrives

A sample recap · Session VII

The Causeway

Read aloud · forty seconds

You rode out of Harrow at first light, the road east past the burned mill. Nobody comes back from the Fen, the ferryman said, and you paid him anyway.

The fight at the causeway was short and ugly. Brannoc went under the water with the thing that had him, and came up alone. Ysolde burned the reeds to get him out.

At the inn you counted what was left: three days of food, one lamp, and the map with the mark on it. Darrow put the lamp out himself.

A sample. Yours is written from the moments your table captured, and read aloud by a narrator who has been taught how to say your names.
