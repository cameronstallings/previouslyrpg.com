Privacy Policy

Previously records moments at your tabletop game and turns them into a recap. That means it handles your voice, and sometimes other people's. This page says exactly what is collected, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

Previously is operated by Cameron Shaw Stallings, an individual based in North Carolina, USA ("Previously", "we"). The app is offered in the United States. Questions and requests go to support@previouslyrpg.com.

What we collect

Your account

An email address, or the identifier Apple gives us if you sign in with Apple, and a display name. We use these to sign you in and to show who is at a table.

Your tables

Campaign names, session dates, the characters you build (a name, an appearance, a crest, optional notes on who they are and how to say their name), the people you invite, and the cast and the loose ends the narrator keeps track of.

Moments, and the recordings behind them

During a session you can tap the capture seal to record a short voice clip, or type a note. Voice clips are uploaded to our storage and transcribed. A moment is recorded only after you tap the seal, and it stops when you tap again or after one minute at most. Separately, the game master can turn on whole-session recording, which is described below: that one keeps recording while the app is in the background and while the phone is locked, until the game master turns it off, until six hours, or until the month's recording time is used.

Because a voice clip at a table can capture other people, the app asks each member of a table whether they agree to be recorded, and shows everyone who has agreed. It is your responsibility to record only people who have agreed. Someone who declines can still read recaps and take part; they just cannot record.

Recaps

The written recap and, when there is one, the narrated audio file, are stored so your table can read and listen to them later. The game master can edit the written text.

Portrait photos

If you choose a photo from your library for your character, we store that photo. Nothing is taken from your photo library except what you pick.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Apple. We never see your card. We receive a record of whether your subscription is active, tied to your account, so we know which tables to unlock.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can tell you when a recap is ready. Turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information and never have.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, each of which only receives what it needs for its job:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (US region).
  • ElevenLabs transcribes voice clips and whole-session recordings to text and produces the narrated audio of recaps.
  • Groq may transcribe whole-session recordings to text instead. It receives the audio and the names in your cast, so it spells them right, and does not keep them.
  • Anthropic writes the recap from the transcribed moments. We send the transcript, the names in your cast, and the character notes you chose to share; not the audio.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription status on our behalf, using an anonymous account id.
  • Apple handles payment and, if you allow it, delivers notifications through Expo's push service.

These providers act under contracts that restrict what they can do with the data. To our knowledge none of them uses your content to train models under our agreements.

Why we are allowed to

We process your data to provide the service you asked for (running tables, making recaps, unlocking what you paid for), to keep the service secure, and, for optional things like notifications and recording, because you agreed. You can withdraw that agreement at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists, so your table's story stays whole. Voice clips are kept so a recap can be regenerated if it fails. A whole-session recording (a Previously Plus feature the game master can turn on only once every adult member still at the table has agreed in the app; it stays off while anyone under 18 is at the table) is transcribed and then deleted as soon as the recap is written, including any part that could not be transcribed; the transcript stays with the session. The game master can delete a session from its recap page, which removes its clips, transcripts and recap for the whole table. Deleting your account removes your sign-in, your recordings and your portraits at once; tables you run pass to the longest-standing player still at the table, or close if you were the only one left. Content you contributed to someone else's table (a recap the whole table shares) stays with that table unless the game master deletes it.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export. Email us and we will send you what we hold about you.
  • Delete. In the app: from the home screen open Account, then Delete account. Your account and your recordings are removed immediately. If you would rather we did it, email support@previouslyrpg.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Share, or stop sharing. A member of a table can share a recap by link. Anyone with the link can read it and hear the narration until a member of the table stops sharing, which disables the link at once. Sharing is never automatic, and the link carries the recap, the session's number and title, the table's name, and the sharer's character name and crest.
  • Stop recording. Decline recording consent for any table; nothing about you is recorded after that.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings.

Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have the rights those laws give them, including to know, delete, and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

Children

Previously is for people aged 13 and over. The app asks your birth date before it lets you in and does not keep it if you are under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data travels encrypted and is stored with providers that encrypt at rest. Access to the database is controlled row by row, so a member of one table cannot read another table's content. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and update the date at the top. Using Previously after a change means you accept it.
